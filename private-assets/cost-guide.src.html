Home automation in India typically costs anywhere from ₹1.5 lakh for a smart apartment to ₹1 crore+ for a fully-wired luxury villa. What you pay depends on four things: how many rooms, which brands, how deep the automation goes, and whether it's a new build or a retrofit.

4. What drives the cost

  • Wired vs wireless — wired (KNX/Crestron) costs more upfront but is rock-solid and best done at construction stage.
  • Number of rooms & points — more switch points, keypads and zones scale the price.
  • Brand tier — the single biggest lever (a Crestron keypad can be 5× a Wi-Fi switch).
  • New build vs retrofit — pre-wiring during construction avoids costly chasing of walls later.
Tip: Building or renovating? Get the wiring planned before the electrical work — it's far cheaper than retrofitting, and it's the only way to do true wired automation.
