Smart-Home Pre-Wiring Checklist

15+ yrs · 600+ projects

If you're building or renovating, the cabling and conduits you lay before plastering decide what automation is possible — and at what cost. Use this room-by-room checklist with your architect/electrician at the MEP stage.

★ The #1 rule: insist on a neutral wire at every switch point. It's what most homes lack and what every smart switch/keypad needs — retrofitting it later means breaking walls.

When to involve us

At the electrical & MEP drawing stage — before any conduiting. We overlay automation onto your drawings (device placement, conduit runs, back-box schedule) so the wiring is right the first time.

Room-by-room

Every room

  • Neutral at every switch box
  • Deep back-boxes (45–60 mm) for keypads
  • 1× Cat6 to keypad/control location

Living / Media

  • 2–4× Cat6 (TV, AV, AP)
  • Speaker cable to ceiling (5.1 / Atmos)
  • Conduit for projector + motorized screen
  • Curtain-motor power + conduit at windows

Bedrooms

  • Keypad back-box at bedside + door
  • Curtain/blind motor power at windows
  • AC indoor-unit control wiring
  • 1× Cat6

Kitchen / Utility

  • Appliance & exhaust control points
  • Water-leak sensor conduit

Bathrooms

  • Leak sensor + exhaust automation wiring
  • Mirror-defogger / geyser control point

Main door / Entry

  • Video-doorbell cabling + Cat6
  • Smart-lock power & conduit

Gate / Outdoor

  • Gate-motor power + conduit
  • CCTV Cat6 home-runs to each camera
  • Facade / landscape lighting conduits

Network rack

  • Central low-voltage cabinet location
  • All Cat6 home-run here + power + ventilation

Electrical panel / DB

  • Space for automation DB & actuators
  • Dedicated circuits for AV, network, motors
